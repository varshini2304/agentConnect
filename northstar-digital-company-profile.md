## Company overview

Northstar Digital is a fictional digital product and consulting studio. It helps small and mid-sized organizations plan and build customer portals, internal tools, data dashboards and integrations.

The sample company's business assistant can explain this service catalogue, collect high-level project requirements and prepare questions for a human representative.

## Business categories

- Digital product consulting
- Web application development
- Data dashboards and reporting
- Systems integration

## Sample service catalogue

### Product discovery

Structured discussions to understand a business problem, its users and possible software solutions. Discovery may include workflow mapping, feature prioritization and a written project outline.

### Customer and partner portals

Planning and development of secure web portals for customers, suppliers or business partners. Requirements depend on user roles, information access and the systems that need to connect.

### Internal business tools

Web applications for tracking requests, managing operational workflows and coordinating teams. Initial discussions should identify the current process and the outcomes the team needs.

### Reporting dashboards

Dashboards that present selected business data in a useful format. Data sources, definitions, update frequency and access permissions need to be agreed during discovery.

### System integrations

Connections between business applications using documented APIs or supported data exchanges. A project discussion should identify the systems, available documentation and required data flows.

## Example technologies

The fictional service catalogue includes these example technologies:

- Frontend: Vue, React, TypeScript and CSS.
- Backend: Node.js, Python and REST APIs.
- Data: PostgreSQL, SQLite and CSV imports.
- Delivery: automated checks and container-based development environments.

These are sample discussion topics. They do not establish certifications, partner status or experience with a particular customer's systems.

## Information for a project discussion

A prospective client can share non-confidential information such as:

1. The business problem they want to address.
2. The people who will use the proposed tool.
3. The main tasks or workflows it should support.
4. Existing systems or data sources involved.
5. Public API documentation, if an integration is needed.
6. Any accessibility, privacy or security requirements already identified.
7. An indicative budget range and preferred timeline, if available.
8. The next step they would like to discuss with a human representative.

Passwords, access tokens, customer records and confidential contracts are not needed for an initial conversation.

- How are retailer accounts approved today?

### Can the agent provide a fixed quote or delivery date?
